## Assistant

We've built Alba from wireframes through a complete clickable prototype with design system. It's an investment app helping members (inclusive, though built for women) invest in what they believe in, with community and learning.

**What we made:**

Five-tab app with onboarding (splash → pace → themes → payment rhythm) flowing into live pages: Home (balance + 10-year projection), Wealth (holdings by type with risk pills), Discover (filter by theme/risk/type across 24 ETFs), Circle (events, followers, posts), Learn (21 videos). All pages use Swissquote CT throughout, outlined stroke containers on white, soft orange accents (#FA5B35 text and borders, #FEDCD3 light fill), soft green (#1E8A5E) for positive performance, and an "Ask Alba" AI chatbot floating button on all tabs.

**Key decisions:**

- Swissquote CT as the only typeface for consistency and elegance.
- Accent orange (#FA5B35) on active tabs, selected cards, pills, and switches.
- Splash screen now has a full orange gradient background with white logo.
- Logo is 2b Horizon (half-disk with rising sun, next to "Alba"), with "by Swissquote" in light thin text below.
- Selected options (onboarding cards, radio buttons, payment pills) use consistent orange border + light fill + checkmark styling.
- "Two years later" is now a modal centred over Home, showing the two-year story (Sep 2024 to Sep 2026, CHF 200/month, CHF 5'260 balance, CHF 29'300 projection).
- All copy uses "members" instead of "women" for inclusivity.
- AI chatbot offers contextual suggestions per page; clears when switching tabs.
